মৌসুমী ঘোষ

Mousumee Ghosh

Mousumee Ghosh writes in Bangla and in Hindi. She was born and raised in Bengal, and the rivers, kitchens and monsoons of that childhood run through most of what she writes.

Her poems lean toward the small and the domestic: an afternoon beside a stove, rain on a brick wall, the long quiet of a house after the guests have gone. She has been writing for decades, mostly in notebooks, mostly for herself.

স্মৃতিরা ভাসে, চলে যায় বহুদূর

Memories drift, and travel far away

About this collection

Poems go up as they are written, and the older notebooks are still being typed out, so the archive grows a little each month. Where a poem carries an English version, it was made within the family rather than by a machine, and it goes after the sense rather than the music.
