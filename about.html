Mousumee Ghosh

Mousumee Ghosh writes in Bangla and in Hindi. She was born and raised in Bengal, and the rivers, kitchens, and monsoons of that childhood run through most of what she writes.

Her poems tend toward the small and the domestic: an afternoon by a stove, rain on a brick wall, the long quiet of a house after the guests have gone. She has been writing for decades, mostly in notebooks, mostly for herself. This site is the first time the poems have been gathered in one place.

About the collection

Poems are added as they are written, and older notebooks are still being typed up, so the archive grows a little each month. Where a poem has an English version, it was made within the family rather than by a machine, and it aims at the sense rather than the music.
