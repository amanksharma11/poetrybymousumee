Write to her

She reads everything that arrives and answers most of it. Write in Bangla, Hindi or English, whichever comes easiest — and do say which poem brought you here.

Email poems@example.com Instagram New poems and photographs

If you are stuck for what to say

A line that stayed with you is plenty. So is a memory a poem shook loose, a question about a word, or a photograph of the place one of them describes.

Translations and reprints

If you would like to translate a poem, set it to music, or print one in a magazine, write and ask. The answer is usually yes, and she likes to know where they end up.
