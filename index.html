How to read here

Every poem carries three ways in. Read it as she wrote it, in Bangla or Hindi. Turn on Pronunciation to see the sound of each line written in the Latin alphabet, so you can say it aloud even without the script. Turn on English for a plain rendering of the meaning.

Listen plays a recording where one exists, and otherwise asks your own device to read the poem in its voice. Any poem can be saved as a PDF or shared with a link.
